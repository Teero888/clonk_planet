Shareware

 

Clonk is shareware. You may freely distribute the game in unregistered and unmodified state.

 

Not all of the game elements are available in the unregistered version. You will need your personal registration code to access all of the scenarios or the round editor options.

 

You may register the game online at http://www.clonk.de or by mailing in the completed registration form Register.txt found in your Clonk directory.

 

RedWolf Design is constantly working to enhance the game, develop new scenarios, missions, and game objects. The latest release of the game is available at the RedWolf Design web site on the internet. Your one-time registration will be good for all regular updates of Clonk Planet.
