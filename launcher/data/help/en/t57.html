The game mode determines the conditions for a round to end successfully. All players that are not eliminated at the end of a round, receive a winning bonus.

 

In cooperative mode, the game ends as soon as the cooperative/single goals set on the right are met.

 

In melee mode, the game ends as soon as all players except one are eliminated. At least two players need to take part in a melee.

 

In melee teamwork mode, the game ends as soon as all players except two are eliminated. At least three players need to take part in a melee. In this mode, two players may attack a third or two teams of two players each may compete.
