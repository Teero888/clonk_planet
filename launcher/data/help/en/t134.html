Mouse Control

 

Mouse control can be added to each set of keyboard controls in the player property dialog.

 

Scrolling

The game viewport can be scrolled using the mouse cursor to view  the complete playing area.

 

Clonk Selection

To select a single crew member, simply left click on the clonk once . To select a number of clonks or those within a building, click and drag a selection frame around the desired crew members. All selected clonks will be marked with a selection frame . With a single right click on a free area, you may also step through all crew members one at a time.

 

Movement

Except when selecting clonks, a single left click on any map area will always yield a movement command. The selected clonks will try to reach the suitable surface closest to the target point. If you click on solid material, this means the closest free surface directly above.

 

In most cases, clonks will find their way through local tunnel systems. The program's search algorithm is limited when it comes to extreme long distances, however, for performance reasons.

 

By clicking directly above the a clonk's head, you can order the clonk to jump to the left or right . The clonks will also try to tackle heights and obstacles by jumping themselves. In some critical landscape situations, you may have to help with an additional movement or jump command, 'though.

 

Digging

You can make your clonks dig tunnels by double clicking into the proper material . The shovel's point marks the bottom center of the area to be dug out. Holding down the Ctrl-key while clicking will make the clonk dig out reuseable chunks of material (i.e. earth or sand) .

 

Activities

Most objects in the game may be manipulated by double clicking on them:

  Enter building

  Grab object

  Let go of object

  Build at construction site

  Chop tree

  Collect item

  Dig out item

 

Moving Objects and Vehicles

Clonks pick up smaller objects automatically, if in range. Common clonks can carry only one item at a time and will have drop or throw away that item before picking up another one. The clonk's inventory is shown in the bottom left corner of the screen.

 

Small items can be moved from the inventory or map to target positions using the mouse drag 'n' drop feature. The selected clonk will try to drop or throw the object at the indicated point. Throwing is possible only if a suitable throwing position is available.

 

By holding down the Ctrl-Key while dragging an item onto a building or vehicle, the item can be moved into that container . You can also select multiple items at a time by drawing a selection frame around the desired objects.

 

By clicking the right mouse button onto a building or vehicle owned by a friendly player, you can view that container's contents. From this contents window, items may be dragged into the landscape as well.

 

Freely moving vehicles (i.e. lorry or catapult) can either be grabbed and shifted by movement command or they can be dragged directly to their target position or into another container (with the Ctrl-Key down).

 

Self-powered vehicles (i.e. elevator, blimp, submarine) can be controlled by simply clicking in the desired target direction.

 

Controlling Objects

Inventory items, grabbed objects, or vehicles and buildings that clonks can operate from the inside can be controlled using the buttons displayed in the bottom right corner of the screen. Here special commands are shown that the selected clonk can carry out independently, too.

 

Creating Construction Sites

Activating a construction kit will open up a menu showing all construction plans available to the player. Structures can be dragged directly from that menu to the desired location on the map. If suitable, the selected clonk will create a new construction site there. You can then make your clonks work at the construction site using the build command . The workers will have to carry the required materials.

 

Multiple Commands

By holding down the Shift-Key while giving mouse orders you can combine several commands to be executed consequently by your clonks. In that manner you can i.e set movement waypoints or move several objects to different positions. Some commands cannot be combined with others.

 

Center Button

Clonk does not make direct use of the middle mouse button. However, if your mouse driver allows you to do so, it is recommended that you assign a left double click to it.
