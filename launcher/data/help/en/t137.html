Line construction and Pumps

 

With line construction kits, power lines and pipes can be created.

 

Lines are connected or disconnected to buildings using a dig double click ⬋ ⬋.

 

The direction of the line is significant. Power lines always need to be built from the supply (i.e. wind mill) to the consumer (i.e. elevator). Energy lines are needed only if ‚Machines need energy‘ is activated in the game’s realism settings.

 

Pipes are constructed to and from pumps. The first line connected to a pump will become the source pipe that will take in liquids and move them to the pump. Drop the open ends of the pipe into liquids with its construction kit box. The second line connected to a pump will become the drain pipe. Drain pipes can also be connected to base buildings. However, you will need some empty barrels in the target building to collect the oncoming liquids.
